About CounselX

A student-built counselling aid for JEE aspirants.

❓ What it is

CounselX turns historical JoSAA opening and closing ranks into something searchable, comparable and easy to understand.

⚙️ How recommendations work

CounselX filters records to exactly one year, round, quota, seat category, gender pool and rank type. It then compares your rank with each record's closing rank using a transparent ratio rule. It is a rule-based comparison, not a trained prediction model and not an admission probability.

🚫 What it does not do

It does not guarantee admission, show fees, placements or locations unless verified data exists, or replace the official JoSAA portal.

🚀 Future scope

Explainable cutoff forecasting with backtesting, verified fee and location data, and a counsellor Q&A mode.

Privacy policy

CounselX collects only what the cutoff search needs. Your profile, shortlist and comparison are stored in your own browser (localStorage) and are not uploaded. The only data sent to the server are the search filters you choose, such as year, category and rank.
